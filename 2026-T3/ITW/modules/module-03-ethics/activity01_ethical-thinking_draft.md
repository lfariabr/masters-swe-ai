# Module 3 · Activity 1 — Ethical thinking (draft)

**Status:** preparatory draft retained for reference. Luis reported posting his Activity 1 responses to the discussion forum on 29 September 2026. This file is not a copy of the final post. These are general scenarios, not accounts of school events.

## 1. Is employing casual staff ethical?

Casual employment can be ethical when flexibility suits the worker and the employer is honest about hours, pay and expectations. It becomes harder to defend when a predictable ongoing need is met through insecure shifts simply to transfer risk to staff. I would ask whether people can decline shifts without penalty, whether rosters are reasonably predictable, and whether the arrangement meets the applicable employment terms. The ethical test is not only whether the contract is allowed, but whether the worker is treated fairly and can make an informed choice.

## 2. May I take discarded office paper for a child's craft activity?

I would not take paper merely because it is in a recycling pile. A page can contain confidential information on the reverse, and office waste may still be subject to a disposal policy. I would first check the workplace's rule and seek permission from the responsible person. Only paper confirmed to be blank and cleared for removal would be suitable. If that cannot be established, I would obtain craft paper elsewhere. The inconvenience is small compared with the risk to privacy and trust.

## 3. Are personal calls or social media acceptable during work?

An urgent personal call is different from repeated social browsing while colleagues cover my work. I would follow the workplace policy, use breaks for routine personal activity, and tell a supervisor if a genuine need affects my availability. The same expectation should be applied fairly across the team. If my role gives access to student or staff information, I must also avoid discussing it in public or posting it online. Respecting time and confidentiality is more defensible than pretending every personal interruption can be banned.
