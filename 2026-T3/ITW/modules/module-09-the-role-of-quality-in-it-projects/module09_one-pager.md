## 🖤 The Big Idea (box it, centre of page)
> **Quality is what you can define, measure and keep improving. If you cannot define it, you cannot measure it, and if you cannot measure it, you cannot control, assure or improve it.**
> (Basu, 2012, p. 5 · Penzenstadler, 2018 · Mahmood et al., 2014)

## 🖤 Zone 1 - Policy, objectives, PDCA (module text)
```
quality policy (management commitment + resources)
   -> measurable objectives per function
       -> every process: PLAN -> DO -> CHECK -> ACT -> (next process's input)
```
- 🔵 QA = the umbrella (policy, programme). QC = the compliance arm (inspect and test products and processes).
- 🔵 Monitor: accuracy · timeliness · dependability · cycle time · KPIs · cost. Control charts show variation.
- 🔴 **Mine:** awards round = rules note (P) · build (D) · review and compare (C) · add rule, rebuild (A).

## 🖤 Zone 2 - Write a quality requirement (Penzenstadler)
| Description | Metric | Measurement | Rationale (why) |
|---|---|---|---|
| responds fast | < 0.5 s | 25 users, use cases 34-35 | else users leave |
- 🔵 Choose a quality model first (ISO 25010).
- 🔵 Qualitative? Break it into countable sub-requirements, or define who rates it (90% rate 9/10).
- 🔴 **Trap:** a requirement without its "why" cannot be traded off.

## 🖤 Zone 3 - Cost of quality (Mahmood et al.)
| Good quality | Poor quality (COPQ) |
|---|---|
| Prevention | Internal failure (rework before delivery) |
| Appraisal (inspect, check) | External failure (after delivery) |
- 🔵 COPQ = lost material + labour hours + machine hours + overhead days, each x rate.
- 🔵 Bridge case: 40.4% → 16.7% of work value once it was measured weekly; productivity +16.9%.
- 🔴 **Mine:** rules note = prevention · zero-difference check = appraisal · Year 11 rebuild = internal failure · the 7-vs-4 email = external failure.

## 🖤 Zone 4 - Reliability and the two axes
- 🔵 **Zhang & Pham:** fixes can add faults (imperfect debugging); test coverage shows how much is left. Release against reliability targets set in advance.
- 🔵 **Zhang:** internal ↔ external focus × objective ↔ subjective measure. Internal quality is the precondition for external (P5).
- 🔴 **Mine:** the Year 12 check after every Year 11 rule = regression guard. R1 (zero differences) = internal and objective; "she calls it fair" = external and subjective.

## 🔴 If you only memorise 5 things
1. **Define → measure → control → improve.** Can't skip a step.
2. **Every process has its own PDCA.**
3. **Quality requirement = description + metric + measurement + why.**
4. **Prevention + appraisal vs internal + external failure.** Failure found late costs most.
5. **Internal, objective quality first;** then the customer's view.
