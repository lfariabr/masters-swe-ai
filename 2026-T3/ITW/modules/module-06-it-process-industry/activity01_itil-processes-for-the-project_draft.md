# Module 6 · Activity 1 - ITIL processes for the project (draft)

**Status:** ✅ posted to the discussion forum by Luis (Oct 2026). Limit is 250 words.

---

My placement project gives school staff one view of student academic results, starting with an awards report. Five ITIL 4 practices (Mohanakrishnan, 2025) fit it best:

1. **Change enablement.** Every change passes a review gate before it is merged, and award rules change only after the academic lead confirms them in writing.
2. **Service validation and testing.** Results are checked against what teachers already see, and tests confirm that permitted roles can open a report and other roles cannot.
3. **Information security management.** The data is about children, so each staff role sees only its own cohorts, every visible field is on an approved list, and anything shared outside the school is de-identified.
4. **Problem management.** When one report view lost rows for a closed semester, the fix was to find the cause and switch to a reliable snapshot. Incident management restores service; problem management stops it happening again.
5. **Knowledge management.** The rules note, runbook and decision log mean the next person, or the next year, can repeat the ranking without guessing.

If I could add a sixth, it would be **relationship management**: the weekly checkpoint with the academic lead is where most rules were discovered.

**Question for you:** which practice do you find hardest to apply in a small team, where one person often holds several roles?
