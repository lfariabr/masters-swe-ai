## 🖤 The Big Idea (box it, centre of page)
> **Engineers send solutions. Communication is making sure the listener received the problem the same way you did, and checking before you send the next one.**
> (Cerri, 2000 · Ferguson, 1977 · Eraut, 2004)

## 🖤 Zone 1 - Content vs context
- 🔵 Engineering habit: **content first** (the answer). Works when the frame is shared; fails across disciplines.
- 🔵 Three systems of representation: **mental · visual · linguistic**. Complete only when all three agree.
- 🔵 Ferguson: engineering thought is largely **nonverbal**. Prose alone drops information.
- 🔴 **Mine:** the rules note was a table and a worked example. The stakeholder corrected a rule in a day because she could see it on a student she knew.

## 🖤 Zone 2 - Informal carries the learning (Eraut)
- 🔵 Most workplace learning is informal: asking, observing, working alongside.
- 🔵 Formal documents record; informal exchanges build understanding.
- 🔴 **Mine:** English floor, latest-result counts, accelerants: every one came from an email, a call or a desk question, not a spec.

## 🖤 Zone 3 - Cerri's seven steps
```
PREPARE                          SEND        CHECK            LOOP
1 setting + mode  ->  2 mirror, pace, lead  ->  3 their map  ->  4 send  ->  5 received?  ->  6 no: back to 1-3
                                                                                              7 yes: next message
```
- 🔵 Steps 1-3 are more than half the process. Understand the system before changing it.
- 🔵 Mirroring = match vocabulary, pace and format, then lead. Not mimicry.
- 🔴 **Trap:** step 5 is the one engineers skip. Sending is not communicating.
- 🔴 **Mine:** the Year 11 email did 1-4 and skipped 5. Her reply showed two misreads ("all clear" was provisional; "like Year 12" meant a wider workbook). A call would have caught both.

## 🖤 Zone 4 - One build, three messages
| Listener | Their frame | What they need from me |
|---|---|---|
| Academic stakeholder | fairness to students | a workbook she can check, one question per email |
| ICT sponsor | risk and ownership | who owns the table after cutover, rollback |
| Reporting coordinator | release timing | which marks are final, when |
- 🔴 Same awards build. Step 3 (their map) decides the message, not the content.

## 🔴 If you only memorise 5 things
1. **Content first is a habit.** Across disciplines, context first.
2. **Three systems:** mental, visual, linguistic. Show, don't only tell.
3. **Steps 1-3 before step 4.** Setting, mirror, their map.
4. **Step 5: check it landed.** Silence is not agreement.
5. **Informal exchanges build understanding;** documents record it.
