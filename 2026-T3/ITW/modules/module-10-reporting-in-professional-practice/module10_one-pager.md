## 🖤 The Big Idea (box it, centre of page)
> **A report is only as good as the agreement it reports against. Write the agreement down, measure against it, control every change to it, and check it with the people it was for.**
> (Smith, 2003 · Blanchard & Blyler, 2016 · de Weck, MIT OCW, 2017)

## 🖤 Zone 1 - Contract and contract review (module text + Smith)
```
customer needs -> contract (what is wanted + what can be delivered)
   -> joint review once drafted -> periodic reviews in performance -> record satisfaction
```
- 🔵 The contract **builds the organisation**: who decides, who designs, who delivers (Smith, p. 1).
- 🔵 Hand over more scope = hand over control. Turnkey: change only by **formal variation** (p. 10).
- 🔵 Disputes: climb the ladder from prevention to negotiation, before trust erodes (pp. 13-14).
- 🔴 **Mine:** no formal contract, but rules note + R1-R4 = the agreement; the academic lead's review rounds = contract review.

## 🖤 Zone 2 - Specification → baseline → change control (Blanchard & Blyler)
| Step | What | Mine |
|---|---|---|
| Need | State the problem in measurable terms | Awards must be reproducible |
| Specification | TPMs, targets and limits | R1-R4 |
| Baseline | Agreed version, then frozen | Accepted workbooks |
| Change | Proposal: problem, impact, cost → board | Rule change → academic lead → rebuild → review |
- 🔵 Informal daily review + formal reviews with owners and a record (s. 5.2-5.3).
- 🔴 **Trap:** skipping informal review to save time pushes the problem into late, costly testing.

## 🖤 Zone 3 - Verification vs validation (de Weck)
| Verification (inner loop) | Validation (outer loop) |
|---|---|
| Built it right? Meets requirements as written | Built the right thing? Meets stakeholder intent |
| Lab, controlled, components | Real use, whole system |
- 🔵 "You can satisfy the requirements, but not the stakeholders."
- 🔴 **Mine:** R1 zero differences + role tests = verification. The academic lead calling it fair = validation.

## 🖤 Zone 4 - Control and accountability (module text + Rubin + Walker & Rowlinson)
- 🔵 Process controls: equipment · documented procedures · skills · **measures of effectiveness**.
- 🔵 **Responsibility must come with authority.** Control = define the task + its metric, then assign it.
- 🔵 **Rubin:** keep a margin for unknowns (reduced gain), integrate in small steps, every change has a cost and an owner.
- 🔵 **Walker & Rowlinson:** goals clear, methods open → fix the outcomes, leave the methods flexible. Trust reduces formal control; registers capture what was learned.
- 🔴 **Mine:** academic lead owns the rules; ICT sponsor owns enabling it for staff; read-only release = reduced gain.

## 🔴 If you only memorise 5 things
1. **The contract fixes who decides.** Scope handed over = control handed over.
2. **Specification → baseline → controlled change.** Every change states its impact.
3. **Verification ≠ validation.** Requirements as written vs stakeholder intent.
4. **Responsibility + authority + a metric,** or it is not control.
5. **Review all the way through,** not just at the end.
