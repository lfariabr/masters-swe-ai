# Module 11 · Activity 1 - Technology career advice: 10 employers, 10 skills (draft)

My placement taught me that I like the work where data meets people: getting a number right, and getting the person who owns it to agree on what it means. That shaped both lists.

**10 employers that excite me**

1. **Kyndryl Australia** - runs the systems other organisations depend on.
2. **Nokia Solutions and Networks** - network technology at scale.
3. **Motorola Solutions** - technology for moments that matter.
4. **Philips Australia** - health technology.
5. **Northrop Grumman Australia** - mission-critical systems.
6. **Mercer Australia** - large-scale data and administration.
7. **Deutsche Bank Australia** - technology in finance.
8. **Morgan Stanley Australia** - strong early-career training.
9. **Department of Education (WA)** - education at system scale, close to my placement.
10. **Otis** - connected devices and service data.

**10 skills that excite me**

*Hard:* (1) SQL and data modelling, (2) Python for data pipelines, (3) cloud platforms, (4) machine learning foundations, (5) secure web development: access checked on the server.

*Soft:* (6) communication, (7) collaboration, (8) the ability to learn, (9) accountability, (10) critical thinking.

Rudder (2019) lists the last five among the soft skills every IT team needs. Doyle (2024) explains why they matter: a hard skill is easier to train than a soft one, and employers now look for hybrid skills.

**How I would start**

- **Pick a lane.** The roles Mallory Careers describes (Grant, 2026) that fit my placement are analytics engineer and data scientist.
- **Show, don't claim.** Doyle (2024) says to demonstrate a soft skill with a concrete case. Mine: I sent a ranking built from the wrong source, corrected it after one phone call, and delivered the next year level in a day.
- **Tell it in STAR form** in interviews: situation, task, action, result.
- **Keep one public project** that shows the hard skills.

**Question for you:** which soft skill was hardest to prove in an interview, and how did you prove it?
