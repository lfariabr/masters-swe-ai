> A fast, hand-write-it-yourself sheet. Built for 3 pens on a blank A4 (landscape).

## 🖤 The Big Idea (box it, centre of page)
> **IT is many jobs now. Hard skills get you listed; soft skills get you hired and kept. List the first, prove the second with a real case.**
> (Grant, 2026 · Rudder, 2019 · Doyle, 2024)

## 🖤 Zone 1 - The map of IT jobs (Mallory Careers)
```
Data · Software · Infrastructure · Security · Business-facing · Support
```
- 🔵 Data: analytics engineer, data scientist, database administrator.
- 🔵 Business-facing: business analyst, systems analyst, consultant, IT manager.
- 🔵 Entry "usually depends on demonstrable skills rather than just qualifications".
- 🔴 **Mine:** data analyst by title; analytics engineer + developer + business analyst in practice.

## 🖤 Zone 2 - Hard vs soft (Doyle)
| Hard | Soft |
|---|---|
| Teachable, easy to quantify | People skills, hard to quantify |
| Degree, language, programming | Communication, teamwork, work ethic |
| **List** it | **Show** it with a case |
- 🔵 Hybrid skills = soft + technical together. Easier to train hard than soft.
- 🔴 **Trap:** writing "I have leadership skills". Say what you did and the result.

## 🖤 Zone 3 - Ten soft skills (Rudder)
Communication · Collaboration · Ability to learn · Creativity · Healthy ego · Accountability · Critical thinking · Empathy · Flexibility · Passion
- 🔵 67% of HR leaders withheld an offer over soft skills (West Monroe, in Rudder).
- 🔴 **Mine:** healthy ego + accountability = correcting a sent result in writing.

## 🖤 Zone 4 - Showing it (Doyle)
- 🔵 Resume: skills section + skills inside each job line.
- 🔵 Cover letter: one soft skill, with a result.
- 🔵 Interview: **STAR** = Situation · Task · Action · Result.

## 🔴 Assessment Hook (bottom red strip)
> **A3** due **Wed 2/12/2026**: §4 can tell the placement in STAR form and name the hybrid skills it built. §2 already says which job I expected and which I did.

## 🔴 If you only memorise 5 things
1. **IT is a map of jobs,** not one job.
2. **Hard = list. Soft = prove.**
3. **Hybrid skills** are what employers want.
4. **STAR** for every interview story.
5. **Being wrong well** (healthy ego, accountability) is a skill.
