## 🖤 The Big Idea (box it, centre of page)
> **A project survives on paper only if the people behind it agree. Write the proposal to sell a specific problem, manage risk as a loop, and never let one voice define the requirements.**
> (Kerzner, 2013, First National Bank · Taylor, 2004, Ch. 7)

## 🖤 Zone 1 - The proposal skeleton (R4 + R3)
```
PROBLEM (specific loss) -> VISION (ties to strategy) -> BENEFITS (measurable)
   -> DELIVERABLES -> SUCCESS CRITERIA (SMART) -> APPROACH -> COST
   + exec summary if > 2 pages   ·   decided in ~5 min   ·   "people buy from people"
```
- 🔵 **Kerzner's version adds:** alternatives, impact on each division, cost-benefit, long-range implications, recommendation.
- 🔴 **Trap:** "efficiency" is not a problem statement. A **specific incident** is (my example: Trial marks missing from the warehouse).

## 🖤 Zone 2 - Why FNB's database failed (R3)
| Process said | Reality did |
|---|---|
| Committee ranks every project | Ranked #1 on one sponsor's word |
| Proposal covers impact on all divisions | Other divisions deferred: "John's baby" |
| Team staffed by negotiation | 2 juniors, no seniors, GM silent |
| Quality review gates | 90%+ of tasks redone, PM removed, project tabled |
- 🔴 **Root causes:** single-voice requirements · **no users on the team** · rushed plan · no escalation (an ethics link to M3).

## 🖤 Zone 3 - Risk as a loop (R2) ⭐ the graded tool for A2/A3
```
Plan -> Identify -> Qualify -> Quantify -> Respond -> Track -> Control -> Archive
          |            |           |           |
   brainstorm,     filter +     EV = Σ p·X   Avoid · Transfer
   fishbone,       top 10 +     NPV, trees   Mitigate · Accept
   assumptions     watch list
```
- 🔵 **Risk** = event + probability + impact. **Business** risk (manage it) vs **pure** risk (avoid or insure).
- 🔵 **Contingency reserve** (planned, PM holds it) vs **management reserve** (unforeseen, executives hold it).
- 🔴 **Assumption analysis:** "fresh = complete" was an assumption, not a fact.

## 🖤 Zone 4 - Pick the method to fit the project (R5)
| Fixed scope, fixed budget | Evolving requirements | Visual flow of tickets |
|---|---|---|
| **Waterfall** | **Agile / Scrum** (sprints, 4 ceremonies) | **Kanban** (pull + WIP limits) |
- 🔵 **Life cycle:** initiate → plan (WBS) → execute → monitor & control (cost · time · scope) → close (retro).
- 🔵 **10 PMBOK areas:** integration, scope, schedule, cost, quality, resource, communication, risk, procurement, stakeholder.
- 🔴 **Brooks's law:** adding people to a late project makes it later, and FNB proved it.

## 🖤 Zone 5 - The people side (R1)

- 🔵 A degree is an **index**: know where to look. Politics is real; learn the landscape.

## 🔴 If you only memorise 5 things
1. **A problem statement needs one specific loss**, not "efficiency".
2. **Requirements from one voice = risk.** Validate with every affected group.
3. **Risk = event × probability × impact.** Manage about 10 at a time and keep the rest on a watch list.
4. **Avoid · Transfer · Mitigate · Accept**, and escalate past your threshold.
5. **Method follows the project:** waterfall for fixed scope, agile or Kanban for change.
