My Assessment 1 is drafted, so I used this module's resources to check what is left and where the gaps are.

1. **State one specific problem, not a general one** (ProjectManager, 2014). My problem statement names one concrete data gap that staff relied on.
   *Challenge:* keeping it concrete without exposing workplace detail in a public journal.

2. **Check the requirements came from more than one voice** (Kerzner, 2013). The First National Bank case failed partly because one sponsor defined the project.
   *Challenge:* my requirements came mainly from one academic lead and my supervisor. I need to show the wider group of affected staff without overstating who I consulted.

3. **List my assumptions as risks** (Taylor, 2004). "Recently refreshed data is complete" was an assumption that turned out to be false.
   *Challenge:* turning a technical issue into a clear risk statement in a few words.

4. **Name the planning method I actually use** (Invensis Learning, 2021). My work runs like Kanban: requests arrive as tickets, and I track releases in Git.
   *Challenge:* describing it honestly without claiming a formal methodology we don't run.

5. **Meet the stated need before proposing more** (Ali & Eagar, 2022). I delivered the reports staff asked for, then proposed fixing the data foundation.
   *Challenge:* my first draft described the project in bigger machine learning terms than the school approved, so I scaled the language back.

**Remaining actions:** final proofread against the brief, check APA formatting, confirm the hours/duties e-journal format with the facilitator, and submit before Sunday.

**Gap I'm still working on:** showing relationship-building as specific actions, not just a statement that it happened.

I'd like to connect with anyone on a data or analytics placement, especially if your project also depends on cleaning up existing data before building something new.
