## 🖤 The Big Idea (box it, centre of page)
> **A business model for an IT project answers three questions before the money moves: which option (decision tree), is it worth it (cost-benefit), and will people use it (acceptance).**
> (Coles & Rowley, 1995 · Ali, 2012 · Degerli & Ozkan, 2014)

## 🖤 Zone 1 - Decision trees (Coles & Rowley)
```
[decide] --large £8m--> (event) --0.3 significant-->  14
                                \--0.7 moderate----->   9      EV = 2.5
         \--small £5m--> (event) ... + option to expand £4.5m  EV = 4.2  <- choose
```
- 🔵 **Forward pass:** decisions, events, order, payoffs, probabilities.
- 🔵 **Backward pass:** EV = sum(p x payoff), right to left; keep the best branch; cross out the rest.
- 🔵 The criterion encodes your risk attitude: EV · equally likely · maximax · minimax.
- 🔴 **Trap:** sensitivity analysis tests robustness. If the answer survives the estimates being wrong, it is a good answer.
- 🔴 **Mine:** workbook first, page later = small plant + option to expand.

## 🖤 Zone 2 - Cost-benefit analysis (Ali)
| Costs | Benefits |
|---|---|
| Purchase: licence, install, support | New features, now and later |
| **Learning:** formal, informal, self | Speed: hours saved |
| Extra hardware/software, tuning | Competitive edge, better process |
- 🔵 Rank options by net gain or benefit-cost ratio. CBA = persuasion + planning tool.
- 🔴 **Trap:** the price is not the cost. Learning and late incompatibility arrive after purchase.
- 🔴 **Mine:** "fewer disputed rankings" is a real benefit that is hard to put a dollar on (Ali's weakness).

## 🖤 Zone 3 - Acceptance (Degerli & Ozkan BPAM)
```
participation (PD) ----------------------------> intention to use (BI)
results you can see (OR) --> useful (PU) + easy (PEOU) --^
culture (OC) --> what colleagues expect (SN) ----^
```
- 🔵 "Acceptance is the key factor that determines whether a project or system is successful" (p. 293).
- 🔵 Granularity = just enough; stability = not changed too often.
- 🔴 **Mine:** the stakeholder corrected the rules note in a day: PD. She checks a named student's row: OR.

## 🖤 Zone 4 - Team vs group (Goodpasture Ch. 4) + forecast vs budget
- 🔵 Crowd → group (purpose, roles, norms) → **team** (common goal, collective commitment).
- 🔵 7 values: trust · commitment · accountability · continuity · simplicity · clarity · certainty.
- 🔵 **Forecast** = best estimate, re-stated as you learn. **Budget** = committed, changed only by approval.
- 🔵 **Balance sheet (concept):** expectations = capability + risk. The gap is risk; name its owner.
- 🔴 **Mine:** my early page estimate was a forecast and it slipped. Say so; don't pretend it was a budget.

## 🔴 If you only memorise 5 things
1. **EV = sum(p x payoff), right to left.** Keep the best branch.
2. **Sensitivity:** does the choice survive wrong estimates?
3. **The price is not the cost.** Learning is a cost.
4. **Participation drives acceptance.** People defend what they helped build.
5. **Forecast ≠ budget.** One moves as you learn; one moves by approval.
