Academic side of lead-cutman (the Student360 × DataWrestler lead in the workplace repository):
use the `itw-corner` skill. Workplace-derived assessment and project prose committed or submitted
from this subject comes from `deidentified/` and passes its leak scan. Original study notes about
public course resources may live under `modules/`, after checking for workplace identifiers and
copied source text.

For work aligning ITW assessments with Student360, read
`notes/student360-plan/control-journal.md` at the start of the session.

Use its deliverable review order, current focus and decision register to resume.
At the end of each substantive session, update the deliverable statuses and next
action, and append dated session release notes with artifacts, actual checks,
user feedback and unresolved items. Follow the journal's session numbering.

Keep historical notes intact and distinguish proposals, accepted artifacts,
implemented changes and deployed releases. Do not infer acceptance, hours or
workplace approval. This workflow does not introduce an additional approval gate
for routine authorized work. The workplace repository remains the operational implementation source
of truth; this journal tracks academic alignment and review progress.

The `notes/` and `_drafts/` directories are local and ignored. On a fresh clone, use the public
`deidentified/` material and do not invent entries for the absent private journal or evidence log.
