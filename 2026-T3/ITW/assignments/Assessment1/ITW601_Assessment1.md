## Assessment Task
Write a 500-word (+/-10%) reflective journal to critically evaluate your WIL placement organisation and determine a potential software solution for a problem the organisation is facing.

## Task Instructions
- **Introduce the organisation:** the type of organisation; the type of project the organisation is working on; the size of the organisation in terms of employees and so on.
- **Detail the structure of your team:** the number of team members; hierarchy of the team; the nature of projects your team is working on such as development, maintenance or operations; what your role is; what the organisation is expecting from you during your placement.
- **Analyse the actions** you have performed that helped towards building better relationship among team members.
- **Provide an explanation about the responsibilities** you have been given and how you have gathered this information; for example, from your line manager, HR representative or your team members.
- **Provide your analysis on the processes and procedures** that are currently in place in your organisation and identify the strengths and weaknesses. Also determine how to optimise the processes and include evidence to support your methods.
  - Propose and discuss a potential software engineering project that may offer solutions to the problems experienced.

## Reflective Journal Structure
- **Introduction** - Background information (75 to 100 words): type of organisation, nature of the industry, type of projects, size in employee numbers and other critical background; information regarding the team you are working within.
- **Insightful Observations and Possible Solutions** (250 to 300 words): overview of workplace requirements and expectations; project planning tools used; relevant ICT infrastructure currently used; recommendations on how to optimise current processes; a possible solution to rectify current problems.
- **Conclusion** (75 to 100 words): how will this informed account assist you in your industry placement interview?

If any sources are used, include in-text citations and references in APA style.
