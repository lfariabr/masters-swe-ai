## Assessment Task
Write a 3,000-word (+/- 10%) Reflective Journal outlining the nature of industry placement positioning and personal reflection on what you have learnt throughout this journey.

## Task Instructions
This final assessment builds on the previous two: first review them, then provide an industry placement outcome and self-reflection.
- Outline the outcomes of your industry placement positioning.
- Describe how your understanding of industry knowledge deepened your personal and professional insights.
- Provide an update in the form of a personal reflection statement on the learning and experiential insights gained, building on the approach adopted in the previous assessments.

## Reflective Journal Structure
- **Introduction** - Background information (500 words): add to or amend the description of the organisation, the nature of the industry and the progress or outcome of the project/s since starting the placement.
- **Industry Placement Positioning** (500 words): type of industry placement - expectation versus offerings; employer's likely expectations in terms of positioning.
- **Project Synopsis Review** (1000 words): based on what you learnt during Modules 9-12, review your Assessment 2 project synopsis and highlight what parts need more work/understanding.
- **Reflective statement** (1000 words): critically reflect on the learning and experiential insights gained, with comparison to credible resources.

If any sources are used, include in-text citations and references in APA style.
