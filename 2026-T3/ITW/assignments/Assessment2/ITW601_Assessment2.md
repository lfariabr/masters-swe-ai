## Assessment Task
Write a 1,000-word (+/- 10%) Reflective Journal. This will be a project synopsis to provide a quick overview of your work for analysis by a stakeholder or project sponsor.

## Context
A project synopsis contains the planned activities required for successful completion of a proposed project and provides an overall view of the work for quick analysis by business stakeholders or project sponsors. The assessment should:
- Include an account of what is required for your interview: professional standards you need to adhere to, and any planning undertaken to understand the organisation's background and requirements.
- Consider what challenges the organisation is currently facing in terms of ICT infrastructure and build a project synopsis to solve the issue.

## Task Instructions
- Describe some challenges a typical organisation faces in terms of their ICT infrastructure.
- Provide your proposed solution to one of the challenges in the form of a project synopsis.
- Describe the type of industry placement interview you are anticipating and the organisation's likely expectations.

## Reflective Journal Structure
- **Introduction** - Background information (200 words): add to the description of the organisation, the nature of the industry and the project/s you have been working on since starting the placement.
- **Interview Preparation** (100 words): type of industry placement interview anticipated; employer's likely expectations.
- **ICT infrastructure Challenges** (200 words): list and briefly analyse challenges the host organisation faces in ICT infrastructure.
- **Project Synopsis** (500 words): proposed solution to one challenge as a project synopsis and why it is critical; motivation, methodology, managerial and technical process plan; all required tools including project management tools; ethical issues you may face.

If any sources are used, include in-text citations and references in APA style.
